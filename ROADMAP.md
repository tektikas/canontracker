# Roadmap

This mirrors the app's public `/roadmap` page. Shipped items are in CanonTracker today (the full list is in [docs/FEATURES.md](docs/FEATURES.md), and the app's `/changelog` page lists every change by day). Next and Later are planned, not built, and carry no dates.

## Shipped recently

- **Try it without an account:** Try the demo opens a demo studio in one click: three sample projects, most of their canon in place as labelled sample canon, conflicts found, a change waiting, and a short review queue to try.
- **A fuller first tour:** review, conflicts and impact, then optionally the wiki, the timeline, Ask, the reader, export and agents.
- **A new version from any file name:** on a document's page, upload "ch3-final-v2.docx" as its next version instead of starting a new one.
- **Archive documents:** put a retired document out of the way without losing its history or the canon that cites it.
- **Revert to an earlier draft:** upload an earlier draft again and it becomes the newest version; the versions in between stay readable.
- **More formats as text:** Fountain screenplays, Yarn and Ink dialogue, CSV and JSON files import as sources like any document.
- **Screenplay cues in script vs recording:** a table read lines up with a script in screenplay format, not only "NAME: line" scripts.
- **The medium, asked up front:** a new project asks whether it is prose, a game or for the screen, so labels and entity types fit from the start.
- **Episodes in natural order:** Episode 2 comes before Episode 10 in the Library and in reading order.
- **Impact across every kind of document:** a proposed change shows what it touches in quests, lore and episodes, not just scenes.
- **Acts after death:** a conflict for a character who speaks or acts after canon says they died.
- **Review by document or folder:** work through the candidates from one chapter or one folder at a time.
- **Open conflicts in exports:** the exported story bible lists the conflicts still open, so readers see what isn't settled.
- **Entity type when adding a fact:** say whether a new name is a character, a place, an item or something else when you add a fact by hand.
- **Big wiki pages for agents:** connected agents read a large character or place page in parts instead of hitting a size limit.
- **Upload a whole manuscript:** one file of a book splits into chapters, in reading order, in a folder of its own. Upload it again and only the chapters you changed get a new version.
- **Reading in the background:** an upload answers at once while the AI reads it, with live status in the Library, on the document and in Review, and a plain reason and Try again if a run fails.
- **Story or reference, told apart:** the AI says whether a document is story or reference notes as it reads, so a glossary filed as "chapter 12" is still sorted as lore.
- **Canon decisions stay human:** connected agents read, check and propose. Accepting, rejecting, merging and deciding conflicts happen only in the app, by a person, whatever the agent's token allows.
- **Six samples to explore:** three original stories, one of them a game, and three public-domain classics: Sherlock Holmes, the Oz books and Alice.
- **A timeline in reading order:** a novel without clock times still gets a timeline: each fact sits in the chapter that tells it.
- **Same detail, two answers:** flags one character with two answers for a detail that can only have one, like a wound in the shoulder and in the leg, with both quotes.

## Next

Planned after the current work. Not built yet.

- **In-world calendars:** your world's own months, eras and years, instead of numbered days.
- **Project roles and secret canon:** give someone a role in one project only, and keep GM-only facts that share links never show.
- **One fact, many sources:** a fact told in three chapters cites all three, so cutting one scene doesn't leave it unsupported.
- **Change sets:** one proposed change that touches many facts, like a renamed city or a moved date, with its impact shown as a whole.
- **More imports:** PDF, Final Draft (.fdx), an Obsidian vault and a zip of many files.
- **Localisation glossary:** every name, alias and term as CSV or JSON, ready for translators.
- **Word export with tracked changes:** export to Word with tracked changes kept, for editors who work there.
- **Names that change over time:** dated aliases and titles: the princess who is crowned in book two stays one person, with the right name for each moment.
- **Who knows what, when:** a rule that flags a character acting on something they haven't learned yet.

## Later

On the list, further out.

- **A series across projects:** canon that carries from book 1 to book 3, or from season 1 to season 2.
- **Maps and places within places:** pins on your own map, and a district inside a city inside a kingdom.
- **Assign review work:** hand a teammate a batch of candidates or conflicts and see what is left.
- **A commenter role:** editors and beta readers comment on documents without changing text or canon.
