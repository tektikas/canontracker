# Characters of Broken Tide

Leya Venn is a cartographer's apprentice who maps the shifting sandbanks around Solen.

Arin Venn is the keeper of the Solen Lighthouse. Leya and Arin are siblings, born to the same mother, and Arin is four years older.

Mara Holt runs boats out of the Harbor and trades at the Salt Market. Mara and Arin were engaged until the storm on the evening of Day 1, when Arin broke off the engagement at 19:00.

Teodor Brask is the last archivist of the Old Archive.
