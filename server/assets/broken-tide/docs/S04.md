# S04 — The Archive

At 13:00 on Day 1, Leya enters the Old Archive looking for an older chart of the sandbanks.

Teodor is asleep at his desk. The lead-lined box is open.

Leya picks up the chord stone with her bare hands and turns it toward the window. The light inside it hums.

She puts it back at 14:00 and leaves without the chart.
