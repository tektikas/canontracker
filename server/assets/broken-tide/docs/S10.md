# S10 — Teodor's Bargain

On the morning of Day 3, Teodor finds the lead-lined box empty and walks to the Lighthouse.

Mara's fiancé, Arin, opens the door at 10:00 on Day 3 and hands over the leather pouch.

Teodor presses the chord stone briefly to the back of Arin's hand, so that he will forget the night.
