# Quest: A Debt in Salt

## Summary

A Debt in Salt is a side quest about money that never quite got paid. Odo Fenn owes Ysolde forty silver marks from a bad winter at the Foot of the Pass. Odo keeps the tally of every lamp he lights and every coin he borrows, and he has not forgotten a single mark. Ysolde has not forgotten either, and she is patient in the way that only a caravan captain can afford to be. The debt is small by caravan standards and large by Odo's. That gap is what the quest is about.

## Giver

A Debt in Salt is given by Mother Brisk once the player has eaten at the Kettle & Coal. She feeds every caravan that rides into Kettlehold, and she hears everything said over her tables. She does not ask for help directly. She says that Odo has been sitting at the back of her bar for three nights, and that she would like him to go home before he drinks what little he has left.

## Trigger

The quest starts when the player speaks to Odo for the first time after the meal. Odo is at his post on Kiln Street, polishing a lamp glass that does not need it. The conversation starts with lamp oil and ends with the debt. The player will notice the oil on his hands before anything else.

## Steps

1. Talk to Odo. He is proud, tired and short of money. He will not say the word "debt" until the player says it first. Let him talk about the winter first. The player learns more from what he leaves out than from the debt itself.
2. Find the tally coat. Odo keeps the record of what he owes in a coat with a torn lining. The tally is written in his own hand, and it shows the sums, the dates and the name of the lender. The coat smells of lamp oil and old wool, and the lining is torn in the same place every time it is folded.
3. Check the tally against the caravan ledger. The numbers only match if the player pays the interest that Ysolde never mentioned. The check is slow and fiddly, and it rewards players who read the margins.
4. Pay or forgive the debt. The player may pay the forty marks in full, or write the sum off and tell Odo he owes nothing. Either choice ends the quest.

## Branches

Pay it yourself, or expose Ysolde. Paying it yourself is quiet and costs the player most of their coin. Exposing Ysolde means taking the tally to the Pass Guard, where Rourke will read every line and ask the same question twice. The caravan's smuggling of ember salt comes out in that scene, and Ysolde will not forgive the player for it. Neither choice is wrong, but each one changes how Odo greets the player the next time they pass Kiln Street.

## Rewards

Odo's trust, which unlocks his lamp-oil discount for every shop run in the town. The discount is small, but Odo keeps his word on it, and that is the point of the reward. Mother Brisk gives the player a free meal on any later visit. Exposing Ysolde adds a line to the Pass Guard's notes, which Rourke reads aloud at the next muster.

## Outcome

Odo either leaves the debt behind him or carries it into the next act. If the player pays, Odo is grateful and a little ashamed. If the player exposes Ysolde, Odo is free of her, but he loses the one lender in town who never asked him for anything else. Odo will remember the choice longer than the player will, and he says so in his last line.

## Writer notes

- VO lines are needed for Odo in each step and for Mother Brisk at the Giver and Outcome.
- Keep Odo's voice clipped and careful. He counts everything, including his words.
- Odo's lines should never name Ysolde's cargo. Let the player connect the ledger to the caravan themselves.
- Give Mother Brisk one line that sounds like a warning and one that sounds like a joke, so the player is unsure which she means.
- Ysolde's ember-salt smuggling must not be stated outright in this quest. Hint at it through the ledger only, because the full reveal belongs to a later scene.
