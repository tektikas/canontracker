# Quest: The Silent Bell

## Summary

The Silent Bell is the main quest of Act 2 and cannot be skipped. For as long as anyone in Kettlehold can remember, the Ashbell has rung at dusk to warn of ashfall and to close the Cinder Bridge for the night. On the second evening of the story it does not ring. The tower is quiet, the bridge stays open, and the whole town starts asking why. The player follows the trail, finds the missing Bronze Clapper, and decides who rings the bell at dawn.

## Giver

The Silent Bell is given by Corin when the dusk bell fails on Day 2. Corin is the Bellwarden, and he does not like asking for help. He asks Ves because she knows the caravan roads, the people who use them, and how to look at a thing without taking a side. He does not say what he fears most. He only says that the bell has never been silent before.

## Trigger

The quest starts at dusk on Day 2, when the bell is due to ring and does not. Rourke is already on watch at the Bell Gate, and his men are shouting up the tower stair. Ves is in the crowd, and Corin finds her before anyone else does.

## Steps

1. Question Rourke. He stood watch at the Bell Gate that night and swears nobody went up the tower stair. He drinks more than he should, so note which parts of his story he repeats and which he drops.
2. Walk Kiln Street. The lamps are lit, and the shopkeepers have all seen something they will not repeat in front of the Pass Guard. Ask them one question each. The answers are short and useful. Each one is a single line, and a good writer will make the player notice which shopkeeper sounds too sure of themselves.
3. Earn Ashsight. The trail is invisible without it. Finish The Well Before Dawn first, or the next steps are blocked.
4. Follow the trail. With Ashsight, the warm prints of one person show on the stones, leaving the road and heading toward the gorge. The trail crosses the Cinder Bridge and then leaves it for the cliff path.
5. Open the Old Mine. The mine has been sealed since the Night of Ash. The Cinder Key opens the Order's seal. Inside, the sign-in board has one entry in an unsteady hand. Odo Fenn signed it. The Bronze Clapper is here too, wrapped in sacking.
6. Return the clapper. Carry the Bronze Clapper back up to the Bell Gate. The Pass Guard will not let anyone else touch it.
7. Ring at dawn. Ves can ring the Ashbell at first light. Ringing it costs the ringer their voice until the next sunrise.

## Branches

Confront Ysolde publicly or privately. The public route is at the Bell Gate, in front of the Pass Guard and the crowd. Ysolde will not deny the debt in public, but she will hate you for it, and Corin will be shamed. The private route is at Saltgate after the market closes. Ysolde talks more freely there, and she offers Ves a share of the ember-salt run to keep quiet. Taking the share is a permanent choice.

## Rewards

Corin's trust, the bell ringing again, and the Bronze Clapper returned to its place. The public route also gives the Pass Guard's respect. The private route gives a purse of coin and a standing offer from the Red Mule Caravan.

## Outcome

Once the clapper is back, the Ashbell rings at dawn on Day 4. Whoever rings it loses their voice until sunrise. The player decides who that is, and what it costs them. If the player chose the private route, Ysolde is at the Bell Gate to watch the bell ring, and her face shows that she expected this. The town remembers both routes, and the next act reads the choice back to the player.

## Writer notes

- VO lines are needed for Corin, Rourke and Ysolde in all branches, and for Odo in the sign-in board scene.
- The voiceless state is a dialogue-system flag. Set it when the Ashbell is rung, and clear it at sunrise. While it is set, the player can only pick non-spoken replies.
- Do not reveal who paid for the theft in the Trigger or the first two steps. The reveal belongs to the Dawn Bell.
- Keep the tower silent in every scene before the dawn. The sound design should be a missing sound, not a broken one, so the player feels the absence before they understand it.
