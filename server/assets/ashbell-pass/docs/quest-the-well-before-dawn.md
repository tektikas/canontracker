# Quest: The Well Before Dawn

## Summary

The Well Before Dawn is a side quest about one night at the Abbey. The Ember Well sits in the lower cloister of Stillfire Abbey, and its water grants Ashsight to anyone who drinks before the sky turns grey. Ves arrives at the Abbey with questions that the Order is not yet ready to answer, and the quest asks her to earn the answers the slow way. The Order keeps the well behind a locked door and a long memory, and it does not give its water to anyone who asks twice.

## Giver

Sister Agnet, the door-keeper at the Abbey gate, is the first to tell Ves that the Abbess will see her. Sister Agnet does not hand out quests. She simply holds the gate open a little longer than necessary, and that is enough for most visitors to understand what is being offered. She has held that gate for thirty years and has never once been thanked for it.

## Trigger

Ves asks the Abbey for help on Day 2 night, after the bell has failed and the town has started to talk. The request reaches Abbess Hesper through the Abbey gate. Ves does not expect an answer, and she is surprised when one arrives so quickly. The reply comes back the next morning with a single line in her own script, and it names the well.

## Steps

1. Hesper's question. The Abbess is ninety years old and has no patience for vague answers. She asks Ves what she is willing to give up to see clearly. The player may answer honestly or with a joke. Hesper notices either way.
2. Walk down to the well. The lower cloister is cold, and the stone steps are worn into shallow bowls. The steps are narrow, and two people cannot pass on them without one standing still. The water is warm enough to steam in the air, and the smell is faintly of ash.
3. Drink before dawn. The water must be drunk before the first light touches the cloister. Hesper's rule is simple, and she repeats it twice in case the player missed it the first time.
4. Learn Ashsight. This is the gameplay tutorial. The player sees heat left by footsteps for the first time, and the view stays for about a day. Use this step to teach the heat-trail mechanic, with one trail to follow and one wrong trail to ignore. Let the player follow the wrong trail once before they see the right one. The lesson sticks better that way.

## Branches

There is one main path. The player may refuse the well, in which case the quest ends with no Ashsight and Hesper says the Order will remember the refusal. A player who refuses cannot later follow the heat-trails in The Silent Bell, and that dependency must be shown in the quest log. Hesper will not ask twice, and the player should not expect a second offer.

## Rewards

The Well Before Dawn rewards the player with Ashsight. The Order also gives Ves the Cinder Key, a warm iron key that opens doors sealed by the Order, and Hesper says it is a loan, not a gift.

## Outcome

Ves leaves the Abbey with sharper eyes and a heavier debt to the Order. Hesper's last line is the same as her first, and the player should hear it as a warning, not a blessing.

## Walkthrough timeline

Use this order when you test the quest end to end: Hesper's question, the walk down, the drink before dawn, then the tutorial. Tamsin appears in the middle of this run as the courier who carries messages between the town and the Abbey.

At 09:12 on Day 3, Tamsin is at the gate of Stillfire Abbey, out of breath.

## Writer notes

- VO lines are needed for Hesper in every step and for Sister Agnet at the Giver.
- Hesper never raises her voice. Write her lines short and dry, and let silence do the rest.
- Ashsight is a gameplay ability. The tutorial should teach the player the heat-trail view, not explain how the Order makes the water.
- Keep the Abbey's rules visible in the scene text, not in a codex popup, so the player hears them from Hesper.
- The drink-before-dawn rule is a hard check. Show the light in the cloister as it changes, not as a number.
