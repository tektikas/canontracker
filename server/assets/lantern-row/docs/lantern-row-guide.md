# Lantern Row: guide

Lantern Row is the glassblowers' street of Vell, a river town that lights itself at dusk.

Ines Calder is the master glassblower of Lantern Row. Her apprentice, Tobin Reed, is fifteen and talks to the furnaces.

The Bell Tower stands at the north end of the Row. Its warden rings the curfew bell at nine every evening.
