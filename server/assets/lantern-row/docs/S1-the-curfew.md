# Scene 1: The curfew

Ines works the last furnace at the Glassworks from 20:00 to 21:00 on Day 1, shaping a lantern for the Harvest Fair.

Tobin sweeps the floor and counts the cooling racks. "Eleven lanterns," he says. "One short."

When the curfew bell rings, Ines banks the fire and sends Tobin home.
